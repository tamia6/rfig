Menus and autosuggestions. Together.

The menu uses what you type. History-based autosuggestions match full commands from the current directory and appear as inline gray text. Browse with the arrow keys and insert with Right. Ctrl+E accepts a suggestion; Enter runs the full suggestion when present, or your typed command otherwise.

Your habits shape the order.

Selections and executions build a score for each command context. Frequently used candidates move up; up to three get circled numbers. Every other candidate remains accessible, and records stay on your machine.

Linux x86_64 and ARM64 are supported. Help probing uses Landlock + seccomp and requires Landlock ABI 3+ (typically Linux 6.2+). Existing completions and history remain available without it. Download packages for either architecture from Releases.

A FEW DETAILS

A few things before you start.

Do I still need zsh-autosuggestions?

No. rfig includes history-based autosuggestions that replace its history suggestion functionality. Remove the old plugin from .zshrc or your plugin manager, then open a new session. Fish integration also disables native autosuggestions in favor of rfig’s directory-scoped history.

Is rfig a Fig alternative?

rfig is an independent, open-source terminal autocomplete project inspired by Fig. It is not affiliated with Fig and does not claim compatibility with every Fig feature or specification.

Does rfig complete every command?

No. Candidate coverage depends on completion definitions, scripts, generators, and cached help available on your system. Some commands may have no candidates or only basic help-derived candidates.

Does rfig use AI?

No. Completion, history suggestions, and usage ranking run locally; no AI service or cloud account is required.

Does rfig import old history or scan my whole computer?

Old shell history without directory information is not imported automatically. Scanning covers your current PATH and common completion directories, not the whole disk. History, selection records, and caches live in ~/.config/rfig/.

What does Enter do when both a menu and suggestion appear?

Enter executes the full autosuggestion when one is present. Otherwise, it executes your typed input without accepting the highlighted menu item. At the end of the line, Right prioritizes the menu selection; Ctrl+E accepts the autosuggestion without executing it.

Why do some commands have no candidates?

A command may have no usable definition, or background analysis may be pending, skipped, or unsuccessful. Check ~/.config/rfig/enrich.status: done means processing ended, not that every command succeeded. Refresh one with rfig analyze <command>. If Landlock is unavailable or restricted on Linux, probing is refused rather than run without isolation.

How do I enable another shell?

Run rfig setup --shell zsh, rfig setup --shell bash, or rfig setup --shell fish, then open a new session of that shell. Setup does not change your default shell, and candidates may differ between shells.
